How we search

Homes
Whole houses or flats to rent, four or more beds, £3,000 to £5,000 a month. Rooms and places that won't take sharers are left out.
Standouts
Up to £4,100, two or more bathrooms, eight minutes or less to a station, checked live in the last three days.
Sources
Instant alerts from Rightmove, Zoopla, OnTheMarket and OpenRent, Marsh & Parsons' website and a daily search of agents' sites. Every advert is rechecked each morning; let or withdrawn homes move to Gone.
Left out
Brixton, Streatham, New Malden, Twickenham, Hounslow, Isleworth, Acton (W3), Notting Hill (W10/W11).
Bills estimate
"With bills" adds a rough £120 each a month for council tax, energy, water and broadband split four ways. It's our guess, not from the adverts; real bills depend on the council and the house.

The areas

  • Putney Riverside high street; District line at East Putney, trains to Waterloo.
  • Southfields Quiet Victorian grid by Wimbledon Park; District line.
  • Earlsfield Small and village-like; trains to Waterloo in about 15 minutes.
  • Wandsworth Two commons and Southside; trains from Wandsworth Town.
  • Roehampton Green and next to Richmond Park; mostly buses.
  • Tooting Lively, cheap eats, the Lido; Northern line at Broadway and Bec.
  • Balham Busy high road; Northern line and Southern trains.
  • Wimbledon Big town centre; District line, trains and trams.
  • Raynes Park Suburban and calm; trains to Waterloo.
  • Clapham Social and busy; Northern line, Clapham Junction close by.
  • Battersea To the river, park and Power Station; Northern line and Clapham Junction.
  • Fulham Smart terraces; District line at Parsons Green and Fulham Broadway.
  • Hammersmith A transport hub with four tube lines and the river.
  • Shepherd's Bush and East Acton Westfield and the Green; Central line and Overground.
  • Chiswick Leafy and riverside; District line and trains from Chiswick.
